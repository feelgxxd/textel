# Code of conduct

textel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is there; in short:

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent. People come here with every level of experience.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with ideas, and say why.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone speaks for
the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/feelgxxd). Reports are read in confidence, and nobody is
treated worse for reporting in good faith.

The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who
keep doing so, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
